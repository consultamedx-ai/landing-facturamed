Fair questions

Questions doctors ask us.

Is invoicing with ConsultaMed legal?

Yes. FacturaMed generates invoices with the technical requirements of Spain's VeriFactu regulation: a chained digital fingerprint, the official verification QR code, legal series and R1 corrective invoices. During the beta, submission to the Spanish tax agency (AEAT) runs in its test environment; we will switch it to production before the obligation comes into force.

Where are my data and my patients' data stored?

On your computer. Transcription and writing happen locally, and records are stored compressed on your device. No clinical data is sent to third-party servers, and you can back up or export everything whenever you want.

What happens if I cancel?

Nothing bad. There's no lock-in and no penalties: cancel whenever you want and export all your data in standard formats before you go. It's yours.

Do I need technical skills?

No. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use ConsultaMed. And if you get stuck, support isn't a bot — it's our technical team.

What is VeriFactu and does it affect me?

It's the Spanish regulation requiring invoicing software to produce tamper-proof records verifiable by the tax agency (AEAT). It comes into force on 1 January 2027 for companies and 1 July 2027 for the self-employed — most private practices. With ConsultaMed, the technical requirements are met from your very first invoice.

Does it work for my specialty?

Yes. EscribaMed doesn't use rigid templates: it writes from what you say, following the usual clinical structure (reason, history, examination, tests, assessment and plan). Dermatology, cardiology, paediatrics, general practice… if you can dictate it, it can write it.
